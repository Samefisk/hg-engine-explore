# Showcase maps: how to view and test

Three maps generated by the [style system](style-system.md) from ASCII
layouts in `data/maps/layouts/`. Every piece of their geometry is copied
from vanilla HeartGold; the tools checked walkability, seams and game limits,
and a melonDS walk-through proved they load and connect. **How they look is
for you to judge.** Note what looks wrong and where; fixes go into the layouts.

## Getting there (normal play, no dev tools)

Continue your save. You start in Cherrygrove City, in front of the Pokémon
Center door. Three invisible warp panels sit two steps south-west of you:

```
            [ Pokémon Center door ]
                     (you)
  .  A  .  B  .  C           A = Twinwood Crossing (541)
     ^ step onto these        B = Mirrorlake Basin (542)
                              C = Echo Cavern (543)
```

From the start: walk **down 1**, then **left 5 / 3 / 1** for A / B / C,
then **down 1** onto the panel. (Walking left along the panel row itself
would step on C first.) Cherrygrove tiles: (559, 394), (561, 394), (563, 394).

In each map you arrive on a plain tile. The way back is the tile **directly
south of where you arrive**: step down once to return to Cherrygrove (you
land one tile north of panel B).

## The maps and what each one tests

### 1. Twinwood Crossing: Route 29 style, 2×2 chunks (64×64 tiles)

| Goal | What was built | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| Scale without seams between chunks | Four chunks solved as one 64×64 area, so the joins at x = 32 and z = 32 were solved like any other seam | Walk the outer loop; the chunk joins run through the meadow and the long east–west corridor |
| A real route | Winding loop path, one-way ledge shortcut (north corridor drops south into the meadow), a pond, four tall-grass fields, tree islands, one connected region | Jump down the ledge row north of the meadow; try to climb back (you can't) |
| A living route | Four Route 29 residents (they say Route 29 lines), both Route 29 signs, an apricorn tree, Route 29 wild encounters in the grass | Talk to them, read the signs, walk the grass |

Scores: 96.5% of tile joins along vanilla edges, 0 route-changing
mismatches, models 26 / 16 / 22 / 10 KB (limit 60 KB).
Most likely visible seams: around (40–47, 24–31) and (16–23, 16–31).

### 2. Mirrorlake Basin: Route 43/44 style, 2×1 chunks (64×32 tiles)

| Goal | What was built | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| A large lake | Lake covering the centre (~30% water) inside a solver-chosen shoreline | Walk the shore ring all the way round |
| A second outdoor biome | Built from the Route 42–44 / Lake of Rage tileset (rock walls, lakes) to test that the system is not Route 29 specific | Compare the look with Route 44 |
| Terraces | Two one-way ledge rows stepping down from the north plateau | Walk north (west end), then jump down both terraces |

Content: Route 44's two signs and its apricorn tree.
Scores: 95.9% vanilla-edge joins, 0 route-changing mismatches, 23 / 20 KB.
Most likely visible seams: in and along the lake, (16–39, 16–23).

### 3. Echo Cavern: Union Cave style, 1×2 chunks (32×64 tiles)

| Goal | What was built | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| A cave tileset | Built from Union Cave's pieces; the system was taught that cave "void" tiles are not floor | Look at the walls and the darkness around them |
| A maze | Serpentine path from the south entrance to a north-east summit room, dead-end rooms east, a side room with an underground pool | Find the summit room (top right) |
| Cave identity | Union Cave's header: cave encounters, battle backdrop, escape rope allowed, a one-way ledge drop into the central chamber | Walk until an encounter; use the ledge in the upper half |

Scores: 95.3% vanilla-edge joins, 100% layout match, 41 / 36 KB.
Most likely visible seams: (16–23, 16–31) and (8–15, 8–15).

## What the melonDS walk-through proved

`python3 -B -m tools.maps.showcase probe` (memory reads only, no screenshots)
on the built `test.nds` with your `test.sav`:

- Each Cherrygrove panel warps to its map's arrival tile, you stay there,
  the loaded chunks report the generated models (`gen_0678`/`gen_0679`,
  `gen_0680`, `gen_0683`), and the return panel brings you back.
- Twinwood Crossing: walking east along the corridor crosses the x = 32
  chunk seam (29 → 35) on the same map.
- Mirrorlake Basin: the terrace ledge jumps 5 → 7 and refuses the climb back.
- Echo Cavern: the ledge drops into the central chamber (10 → 12).

The new maps use an empty level-script bank (vanilla bank 399). The first
build reused Union Cave 1F's, whose story trigger walked the player one tile
east and locked input on arrival; the probe caught it.

## What was not checked by tools

- How it looks: textures at seams, lighting, anything visual.
- Wild encounters in the new maps: the headers use the template routes'
  encounter tables, but hg-engine's overworld Pokémon spawning is keyed by
  map ID and does not list 541–543, so overworld Pokémon may not appear.
- Map names on screen show the template's location (Route 29, Route 44,
  Union Cave).

## Regenerate or remove

```bash
python3 -B -m tools.maps.style generate --layout data/maps/layouts/541-twinwood-crossing.txt --map 33 --land 676 --seeds 12
python3 -B -m tools.maps.style generate --layout data/maps/layouts/542-mirrorlake-basin.txt --map 46 --land 680 --seeds 12
python3 -B -m tools.maps.style generate --layout data/maps/layouts/543-echo-cavern.txt --map 107 --land 682 --seeds 16
python3 -B -m tools.maps.showcase write     # panels, return warps, NPCs, signs
python3 -B -m tools.maps lint
python3 -B -m tools.maps.showcase probe     # after a build: walks every panel in melonDS
```

To remove everything, delete `data/maps/` entries for land 5, 676–683,
events 64 and 491–493, matrix 288–290 and headers 541–543, then rebuild.
