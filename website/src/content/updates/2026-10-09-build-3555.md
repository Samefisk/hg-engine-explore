This build closes out the current batch of reported issues.

![Riding Stantler in Cherrygrove while a wild Pokémon notices](/shots/riding-stantler.png)

**Fixed**

- Throwing now holds up across long repeated runs, with the thrown Pokémon staying healthy and accounted for.
- Spawning no longer causes hitches while you walk.
- A crash path in the throw effect is guarded against.
- Pokémon that stop after noticing you, such as Geodude and Mankey, resume normal behavior.
- Followers face a sensible direction when they stop.

**Still open**

- Player HP healing, zero HP and battle interaction.
- A rare long-session lock that has not been reproduced.
- Ride and rooftop checks.

Reports on any of the above are welcome.
